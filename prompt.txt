PRODUCT DEFINITION

TeachIt is a digital teaching workspace for teachers.

Its core workflow is:

Class → Subject → Academic Session → Week → Topic → Lesson → Teach → Mark Taught

The core jobs are:

1. Plan — know what to teach.
2. Prepare — prepare/review the lesson.
3. Teach — use the lesson while teaching.
4. Track — record what has been taught.
5. Continue — understand what comes next.
6. Revise — see previously taught material relevant to the current lesson.

TeachIt should feel like a well-organized digital teacher's notebook, not a generic SaaS dashboard, school-management system, or AI chatbot.

The teacher remains in control.

AI is NOT part of the immediate refactoring task.
